# Compliance notes

- Phase 1 uses only de-identified public data. No PHI in this repo, ever.
- Before touching any agency data: signed data use agreement and Business Associate Agreement.
- PHI lives only in HIPAA-eligible infrastructure under a BAA (encrypted at rest and in transit, access logging, least privilege).
- Follow NEMSIS research dataset terms: no attempts to re-identify patients, agencies or hospitals.
- Not legal advice — confirm requirements with counsel and NYS DOH Bureau of EMS.
